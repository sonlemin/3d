Before asking Sonlm to choose how a mechanic should change, first state its purpose and what is physically happening, sourced from the GDD and its decision log.

**Why:** On story 1.6 (2026-11-22) I offered options to fix the gap crossing's "walking midair" feel; Sonlm stopped the question to ask "What is the point of the first crossing. Is the character supposed to walk through missing section". The real issue was a GDD gap (nothing to hold), which the options had missed. Once explained, Sonlm picked a fix that addressed the cause (a rope left across the gap).

**How to apply:** When playtest feedback says something feels wrong, grep the GDD and decision-log for the rule's origin and intent, explain it in two or three lines, name any gap in the documents, then ask. Related: [[user-prefers-option-lists]], [[recommended-option-is-the-decision]].
