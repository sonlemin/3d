In facilitated workflows (brainstorming, game brief, GDD, any guided BMad session), give Sonlm concrete option lists to choose from instead of open-ended questions or fill-in-the-blanks. They said: "You fill them in as a list. I decide." They usually skip open questions and often answer "All of them".

**Why:** Sonlm prefers to act as the decision-maker and curator rather than draft content themselves.

**How to apply:** Turn every question into numbered or lettered options (they can always bring their own idea). Treat "All of them" as accepting the whole list. When options exclude each other, mark one *(recommended)* and say up front that answering "all" makes the recommended one the default while the others become modes. Sonlm answered "all" to every such list in the 2026-11-12 brainstorm, and announcing the rule in advance kept the session moving without extra questions. Don't enforce workflow rules like "your ideas lead" or "max 3 sparks" when they conflict with this preference. Before finalizing a long session, give a condensed set-by-set overview with IDs so Sonlm can scan for changes; they asked for exactly that before closing the 2026-11-12 brainstorm.

**Format (2026-11-12):** during the game brief, Sonlm asked "Can you make it to a choice selecting window", so put the option lists in the AskUserQuestion dialog instead of plain text. The window takes at most 4 questions with 2–4 options each, so split longer sets across calls. It can hide the text written before it, so each question has to stand alone; put drafts up for approval in an option's preview. The built-in "Other" field replaces the "all" shortcut and free-text edits.

**Multi-select needs a "None of these" option.** On 2026-11-12 Sonlm couldn't submit a skills checklist because none of the options applied ("I can't really do those things"). They had to reject the whole window to say so, which held back their answers to the other questions in it. Whenever every option could be false, include a "None of these" option or make the question single-select.
