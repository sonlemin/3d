On 2026-11-19, Sonlm closed brief gate 1 (the ear test) on their own sessions and skipped the five outside testers. Next is planning gate 2, the one-night game. Details are in `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/ear-test-outcome.md`.

**Why:** Direction passed (95.8% on headphones, 87.5% on speakers). Level only fails when cry kinds are mixed across levels, which the game never does. Sonlm judged five outside testers not worth it before the one-night game.

**How to apply:** When planning or building the one-night game:
- Keep one kind of cry per level.
- Keep the rear-shadow front/back cue and per-clip loudness matching.
- Make its first playtests check that newcomers can place threats by ear, on headphones and on speakers, before building more content on that.
- If they can't, use the brief's fallbacks: "best with headphones" plus visual cues, or Plan B (the lantern scouts).
