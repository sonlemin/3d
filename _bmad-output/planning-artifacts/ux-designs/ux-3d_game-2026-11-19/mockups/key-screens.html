Key screens in the Lantern light look

Six screens drawn to DESIGN.md's tokens: semibold type, nothing smaller than the 28 px label size on a 1080p canvas, one warm thing per group, marked by a glow and a thin rule as well as by colour. Each frame is a 1920 × 1080 canvas scaled down to fit this page, so text here appears smaller than it will in the game.

The plan is the same worked example as before: 4 planks and 1 rope in hand after a trapdoor and a swing-beam. The name, the epitaph and the journal hints are stand-ins until the real texts are written.
