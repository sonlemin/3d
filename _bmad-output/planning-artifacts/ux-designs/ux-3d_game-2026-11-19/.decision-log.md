- UX-1 — **Night text: one named exception.** The ally's spoken lines are captioned on screen, always, like subtitles. Nothing else is drawn over the night view. Resolves extract F1; consistent with GD-26. (Declined: captions as a setting; a diegetic signal in place of text.)
- UX-2 — **Hold progress: hands and sound only.** The action is its own progress (counted hammer blows, rope coming in hand over hand, a plank lifting), each with its sound and a clear final beat. No bar or ring. Gray-box builds use simple stand-in hands. (Declined: on-screen ring; ring as a setting.)
- UX-3 — **Ears only switches off pointer cues only.** Off: shaking rail ropes, swirling fog, falling pier dust, leaning flames (toward cries and toward the hum), ropes swaying to the tune. Still on: captions, the wound's darkened side, chalk tallies, bowing planks, anything simply seen in the light. Resolves extract F2/F3: stream-safe's "always shows calls as text" is then a restatement, not a separate case. (Declined: also dropping captions; dropping everything removable.)
- UX-4 — **Key actions are learned; key card on the pause screen.** No prompts at night for E, F, 1, 2, R. Taught once (dusk lesson and planning) and listed on a key card on the pause screen. (Declined: on-screen key prompt; prompts as a setting.)
- UX-5 — **Aim mark: none by default, optional centre dot.** The hands move to a ready pose when something usable is within reach; that is the aim feedback. Settings offer a small centre dot, off by default (motion comfort, precision). (Declined: no mark and no setting; always-on dot.)
- UX-6 — **Cancel and can't: hands and sound, no text.** Cancel: the hand lets go and drops back with a slack or dropped-tool sound. Impossible: the hand reaches, stops short and opens empty, with a dull knock. Applies to keys as well as clicks (UX-4). The reason is read from the forearm tallies or the object. (Declined: a warden caption; no feedback.)
- UX-7 — **Oil is read from the flame and the glass.** Each lantern has a glass reservoir whose level is visible up close; near the end of the tank the flame shrinks and sputters audibly. Look and sound only: reach does not change. A shuttered lantern glows through its seams; an empty one is dark. No numbers. Resolves extract F19. [ASSUMPTION: "near the end" starts at the last 60 s of a 6 min tank; tuning value.] (Declined: oil on the forearm; sound only.)
- UX-8 — **Plank count is drawn as planks.** A section with n planks shows n planks laid in a fixed order, beams and the dark visible through the gaps; seen only where light falls. The carved name shows through once 4 or fewer remain. Positions stay cosmetic (GDD: a count, not positions). (Declined: notches on a post; per-section counts on the forearm.)
- UX-9 — **Build target: stand, face, look.** At a section's town-side edge, facing out, the hands take out the tool; that ready pose is the sign that 1, 2 and R will act on the section ahead. A swing-beam hangs from the rail on the side the warden is looking toward when the hold starts. In planning the rail itself is clicked. Resolves extract F12. (Declined: nearest rail; tap 2 to switch.)
- UX-10 — **Reading the name: look at it in the light.** Name lit and within 2 m, looked at for about 1 s, is read; no key. The forearm rises once on its own to show the name being inked, with a sound, then drops. Afterwards Tab shows it. Resolves extract F26. [ASSUMPTION: 1 s dwell is a tuning value.] (Declined: E to read; instant when lit.)
- UX-11 — **Forearm raised: walk and look, hands busy.** While Tab is held the warden walks and looks freely, the night keeps running, nothing is muffled; neither hand can start an action until release. Tab is ignored during a hold. Resolves extract F24. (Declined: stand still to read; fully free.)
- UX-12 — **Lost night: dark, then the journal page.** No "game over" title. The cries cut to wind, the view goes dark, the journal opens on the new hint being written in; one action continues to planning. The GDD's 3 s replay target is measured from that action. (Declined: straight back to planning; a cut-down ledger.)
- UX-13 — **Journal: planning and pause.** In planning it sits at the edge of the view and opens by click or J; it is also a page on the pause screen. It does not exist until the first hint is written. Not carried at night. Resolves extract F20. (Declined: lost-night moment only; also carried at night.)
- UX-14 — **Dawn ledger: a page beside the bridge.** The view rises over the span as the sky pales; after the silence a ledger page is laid at one side and its seven lines are written in one at a time, by hand, with the bridge still visible. Then two choices: another night, or leave. Resolves extract F21 (form). (Declined: full-screen page; lines written onto the scene.)
- UX-15 — **Planning shows the span plus a tool tray.** On the span: a strength number by each section (plus the GDD's rig coverage and lantern reach). In a tray: planks and ropes in hand; tools 1 trapdoor, 2 swing-beam, 3 lantern with cost and lanterns left to place; chosen tool highlighted; an unaffordable tool dimmed; pointing at a section says why a tool cannot go there. Layout to be wireframed. (Declined: span only; tray plus a night forecast.)
- UX-16 — **Planning click strips the next plank.** With no tool chosen, any click on a section's deck removes one plank in the fixed order the night uses; the strength number drops by one. A click on a rail strips that rail. Pressing the chosen tool's number again, or Esc, puts the tool away. Resolves extract F13. [NOTE: Esc in planning therefore puts a tool away first and opens pause only when no tool is chosen; confirm at IA.] (Declined: strip the clicked plank; plus/minus controls.)
- UX-17 — **Undo: right click plus Start over.** Right click undoes the last choice on the section under the cursor (GDD). A "Start over" control, held briefly, restores the whole span to dusk. Resolves extract F14. (Declined: right click only; global Ctrl+Z.)
- UX-18 — **Start the night: hold Space for 1 s.** Matches the 1 s bell pull. The tray shows the control. The warden's bell rings; the town's bell answers with the single toll of wave one. Resolves extract F15 (which bell). GDD key map says "Space"; this makes it a hold. (Declined: tap; tap then confirm.)
- UX-19 — **Headphones-or-speakers question: kept and recorded.** One screen at first launch, changeable in Settings. Changes no cue. Written to the playtest log; speakers get a one-line "best with headphones" note; ready for the ear re-check fallback. Resolves extract F9. (Declined: drop it; fold into the left/right check.)
- UX-20 — **Left/right check: two choices on a dark screen.** Before the first-person view opens: dark screen, one bell stroke, "Left" and "Right", answered with A or D, arrow keys, or a click. Two wrong answers offer the swap and the single-channel warning (GDD). (Declined: turn to face; walk toward.)
- UX-21 — **Lesson skippable once completed.** After the lesson has been finished once on this machine (a flag kept with settings, not a save game), Start offers "Skip the lesson". Skipping also skips the left/right check, which stays in Settings as "Check left and right". First-timers always get both. Resolves extract F8. (Declined: always skippable; never on a fresh launch.)
- UX-22 — **Controls taught by prompts at dusk plus a key card.** One-line key prompts are allowed during the dusk lesson only: look and walk first; after the three cries a short hands-on part (strike, strip a plank, hang and shutter the lantern, raise the forearm). The key card is on the planning and pause screens. **GDD impact:** adds about a minute to the 2 min lesson; GDD session shape and epic 5 need an update. First cry still inside 60 s of Start because the hands-on part comes after the cries. (Declined: key card only; first-use prompts in wave one.)
- UX-23 — **Title menu: Start, Settings, Credits, Quit.** Start leads to the first-launch questions or, for a returning player, offers "Skip the lesson". Credits carries asset licences and the AI-use disclosure. No Continue (no save). (Declined: a separate "Listening lesson" entry; Start and Quit only.)
- UX-24 — **Pause: full stop, five entries.** Clock, the dead and every sound freeze and fall silent. Available in lesson, planning, night and dawn. Entries: Resume, Key card, Journal (once it exists), Settings, Leave the bridge (asks first; nothing is saved). The playtest build logs each pause. Resolves extract F25. (Declined: night keeps running; no pause in the listening round.)
- UX-25 — **Settings: GDD set plus basics.** Sound: volume, headphones or speakers, swap left and right, check left and right, stream-safe sound. Cues: one three-way choice (In the light [default], Everywhere, Ears only); centre dot. Controls: mouse sensitivity, invert look. Display: fullscreen or window, resolution, fog quality, caption size. No key rebinding, no hold-to-toggle (noted as an accessibility gap accepted at hobby stakes). (Declined: adding rebinding and hold toggles; GDD set only.)
- UX-26 — **Captions: bottom centre, plain line.** One line, no speaker name, shown while the line is spoken and at least 2 s, one at a time. Says exactly what the voice says: no arrows, no direction. (Declined: placed toward the town pier; top centre.)
- UX-27 — **Cues everywhere: the cue itself glows faintly.** Only the cue objects (shaking ropes, swirl of fog, falling dust, ropes swaying to the tune) are drawn in a pale light of their own; nothing else in the dark is revealed; flames lean toward cries beyond their own light. (Declined: screen-edge markers; lighting the whole span.)
- UX-28 — **Toll cue: flames dip, in Cues everywhere only.** With that setting on, every lit flame dips once per toll. Default play keeps the GDD's tally board only. Resolves extract F16. (Declined: flames dip for everyone; tally board only.)
- UX-29 — **Any light shows cues.** The rule is "cues show wherever light falls": the three lanterns and the town gate. Section 6's cues and the tally board are always readable. The gate light cannot be shuttered or moved. Resolves extract F4. (Declined: lanterns only.)
- UX-30 — **A window going out makes a small sound from the town.** One short, quiet sound per window, from the town side; its visual cue is the window itself. Well under the cries; not counted in the four-cry limit. **GDD impact:** one new sound for the asset list. (Declined: silent; an ally line.)
- UX-31 — **Voice: spare and in-world, plain settings.** Short, quiet declaratives in the warden's register ("Ring the bell", "Leave the bridge", "Another night"; the GDD's "The fallen will return."). Settings and system text stay plain and literal ("Mouse sensitivity", "Swap left and right"). (Declined: in-world everywhere; plain throughout.)
