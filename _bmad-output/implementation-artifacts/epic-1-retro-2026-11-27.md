## What went well

Sonlm picked all four of these as habits to keep:

- **Show-me setups run from the Godot editor.** Every manual check had a setup that put Sonlm in exactly that situation, on the laptop's own clone.
- **Option lists to pick from.** Decisions came as choices with one recommended, at story creation and at review. Sonlm chose the recommended option every time the records say which.
- **Look choices shown as pictures.** Pier depth (1.2), pier darkness (1.3) and the read-out (1.7) were rendered and chosen from what Sonlm could see.
- **The bridge falling.** Story 1.5's sag, groan and fall delivered the epic's goal in play.

Also from the records:

- **The sim held up.** Seven stories built on one `NightSim` without a rewrite. When 1.6 replaced a choice from 1.4, the change stayed contained. The bot nights check every tick that parts are conserved and that the same seed gives the same events. A bot checks with its own arithmetic, so the rule under test is never its own judge.
- **Undo each rule (from story 1.4).** Each rule is taken out of a copy of `game/` and the tests must go red. During dev, 1.4 found two holes. Review counts were 18/18 in 1.4, 25/25 in 1.6 and 59/59 in 1.7; the 1.7 pass caught a line that did nothing.
- **Screen checks on a virtual display.** Frames are captured and judged headless, with a scratch input driver from 1.4 and a 1080p capture with pixel-edge measurements in 1.7. They caught the half-pixel blur of 1.7's digits.
- **CI stays strict.** It fails on any engine warning, and the purity check tests itself.

## What was hard

1. **Tests that could not fail, and branches no test reached: 7 of 7 stories.** Examples are 1.3's mouse-capture test and `refuse()`'s side branch (DW-37). The review caught them every time. They grew fewer once the undo pass became routine, but story 1.7 still had four tests tightened at review.
2. **The record said more than was checked.** In 1.1 and 1.3 a manual check was marked verified without Sonlm running it, or from one headless frame. Six commits exist only to correct the record. The dev-story override now forbids reporting sound, feel and similar checks as verified.
3. **Feel and readability were found only in play.** This hit 4 stories on readability (1.2, 1.3, 1.5, 1.7) and 2 on slowness (1.4 holds, 1.6 moves). Three items are about whether a newcomer will understand: the aim, the backward slide and walking on air (DW-41, DW-45, DW-46). `DESIGN.md`'s sizes and colours were too small and too dim on Sonlm's 13 to 14 inch laptop (DW-54).
4. **Rules no document had: 5 of 7 stories** (1.2, 1.4, 1.5, 1.6, 1.7). One conflicts with `EXPERIENCE.md` (DW-39); the read-out's marks are not in `DESIGN.md` (DW-52).
5. **The hours log was never filled.** `epics.md` says hours are logged from epic 1, and DW-18's re-guess of epics 2 to 5 depends on it. The per-story line was blank seven times. That is a process problem, not anyone's fault.

## Key insights

1. In this project, feel can only be judged in Sonlm's own play. Epic 2 is about sound, and agents cannot hear, so this weighs even more there.
2. A check that is not written into the process gets skipped. The undo pass lived in a memory note, not in `project-context.md`.
3. Showing beats describing. Every look choice made from pictures held up.
4. The architecture's foundation is sound: the sim, the tick order, the bot, `--start` and `--show`. Epic 2 adds to it rather than reworking it.
